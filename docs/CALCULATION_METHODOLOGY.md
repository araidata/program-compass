## Implemented in the capability slice (2026-11-24)

`src/lib/domain/capabilities.ts`, unit tested. Gap = target − current when both are set and the topic is applicable, otherwise unknown. Averages are unweighted means over assessed applicable topics (targets over applicable topics with a target); unknowns are counted separately, never as zero. Levels to close = sum of positive gaps. KPI status: higher is better passes at ≥ pass and fails below fail; lower is better passes at ≤ pass and fails above fail; between is at risk; no value is no data. Review: due at six months, overdue at twelve, by calendar month. A topic shared by pillars counts once per pillar and once in the enterprise. Weighting, cadence and deficiency threshold are provisional (DECISION_LOG C-04).

## Implemented provisional business readiness — 2026-11-24

`src/lib/domain/context.ts`, method `reference-readiness-1`. Deduplicate capability IDs; exclude capabilities explicitly marked nonapplicable; count missing assessed current values separately. No mappings = Unmapped; mappings but no applicable capability = Not applicable; any applicable missing observation = Incomplete. Otherwise minimum ≥3 yields Defended (indicative), minimum 2 yields Partly defended, lower yields Exposed. Display minimum and unweighted mean, with the denominator restricted to assessed applicable records. These labels are provisional planning indicators, never measured probability or verified compliance.

An objective's capability set is the union of its explicit required capabilities and defending capabilities of active threats linked to that objective. Inactive threats never change readiness. Multiple joins do not multiply a capability's contribution. No L×I risk score is calculated. Database constraints/API validation bound optional likelihood/impact estimates to 1–5 and require an attributed estimate basis/planning consequence.

## Workforce demand/capacity method — editable provisional inputs

Each unique activity supplies an annual volume, frequency, hours/unit, fixed/project hours, growth multiplier, distinct outsourcing/automation reductions and productive hours/FTE. No default is invented. Gross = `(volume × frequency × hours/unit + fixed + project) × growth`; demand = gross − reductions, with reductions bounded by gross. Workload FTE = demand / sourced productive hours/FTE; unknown denominator means unknown FTE, not zero. Mapping to several requirements/capabilities/NICE elements never multiplies demand.

Actual capacity = sourced annual productive hours × responsibility allocation FTE × inclusive assignment-days / calendar-year-days. Paid capacity uses entered paid hours separately. Vacancies/proposals and missing/inactive/mismatched incumbents contribute no actual capacity. Run capacity = productive capacity × entered run share; change = remainder. Overlap allocations are limited per position and person; adjacent inclusive end/start dates on the same day overlap. Missing inputs yield known subtotals plus incomplete warnings, never complete totals.

Coverage constraints use distinct active qualified assignees and current skill observations on the selected date; minimum coverage/continuity/expertise are not additional additive workload requirements. Separation-of-duties flags overlapping assignees across specified responsibilities. Position justifications union unique linked services; their results cannot be summed across overlapping proposals. Record source, effective date, provisional flag/revision and retain exact captured values.

## Five-year financial arithmetic

Amounts are exact decimal strings persisted as Decimal. Each fiscal year has12 monthly slots beginning in the saved fiscal-start month. One-time costs occur in activation month; recurring annual amount/12 applies in each active inclusive month. Anniversary-year escalation is `amount × (1 + percent/100)^floor(monthsSinceActivation/12)`. Salary/licensing default to the scenario's captured method percentages; other categories may use an explicit line override (zero provisional change otherwise). Savings negate the entered positive amount. Round each line/year to2 decimals, then sum displayed line amounts; cumulative total sums annual totals. No currency conversion.

Existing resource recurring estimates are horizon-base values unless explicitly overridden; do not compound from an invented historic price year. Primary source identities are deterministic; explicit overrides replace rather than add. Additional cost/savings identities must be distinct and sourced. Scenario timing shifts preserve cost offsets from the shared resource activation anchor. Initiative implementation end is not a service termination; recurring costs continue unless their own end is supplied. Resource retirement cuts off position/technology cost and exposes savings as baseline-to-scenario difference. Do not also enter the same saving as a second line. Unknown amounts/dates and mismatched currency mark incomplete totals with line-level reasons; known subtotals are not complete budgets.
