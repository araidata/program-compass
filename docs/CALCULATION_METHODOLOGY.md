# Calculation methodology — preliminary, unimplemented

Proposals require domain review. Keep authoritative calculations outside presentation, deterministic, versioned and independently testable. Inputs require units, period, source, owner and effective date. Missing/invalid inputs produce an incomplete result, never silent zero. Do not invent benchmarks or task durations.

| Calculation                       | Proposed method                                                                                                                                                              | Edge cases and limitations                                                                                                 |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Maturity gap                      | target minus assessed current score on the same scale version                                                                                                                | Unassessed remains unknown; retain negative gaps; aggregation weights require approval                                     |
| Requirement coverage              | Configurable documented threshold across applicable mapped capabilities                                                                                                      | Separate unmapped, unassessed and non-applicable; never verified compliance; aggregation policy pending                    |
| Annual workload (hours/year)      | volume/occurrence × occurrences/year × hours/unit + fixed recurring hours + annual project hours; apply growth then subtract non-overlapping outsourced and automation hours | Document input basis; reject negatives/excess reductions; count unique activities once across frameworks/roles             |
| Productive hours (hours/FTE/year) | paid annual hours minus leave, holidays, training, administration, meetings and other non-overlapping deductions                                                             | Editable assumptions; zero/negative denominator invalid; paid hours are not productive hours                               |
| Required FTE                      | net annual workload / productive annual hours per FTE                                                                                                                        | Retain precision; proposed display two decimals; no automatic whole-position rounding                                      |
| Available capacity                | productive hours × allocation fractions, plus separately attributed service capacity                                                                                         | Do not deduct outsourced hours and credit the same service capacity twice; reject overlapping allocations                  |
| Capacity gap                      | required hours minus available hours                                                                                                                                         | Report deficit/surplus separately; coverage, continuity, expertise and separation of duties remain independent constraints |
| Annual budget                     | unique one-time costs in scheduled years plus recurring costs for applicable years                                                                                           | Separate baseline, approved, proposed and scenario costs; currency/proration/escalation/rounding require approval          |

Compare workload, capability needs, minimum coverage and sourced benchmarks without summing overlapping needs. Position recommendations must explain title, pillar, year, responsibilities, NICE roles, gaps, workload/capacity/FTE, drivers, cost, alternatives, deferral and assumptions. NICE supplies roles/tasks/knowledge/skills, not staffing ratios or standard durations.

Three/five-year views share one plan; years four/five disclose uncertainty. Mandatory deadlines constrain scheduling. Initiative completion cannot automatically change assessed maturity or verified compliance. Future tests cover missing inputs, shared mappings, zero capacity, excessive reductions, recurring costs, scenario isolation, deadlines and reproducibility. Risk scoring, thresholds, weights, currency and rounding remain unapproved.

Source materials required: actual CJIS Security Policy 6.0; authoritative NIST CSF 2.0 and NICE versions; later workforce datasets; measured or attributed organization estimates. No framework datasets or business calculation engine are implemented in Phase 0. AI is future assistance and never authoritative calculation logic.
