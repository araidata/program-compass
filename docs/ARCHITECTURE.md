## Workforce demand and capacity — 2026-11-24

Strategic objective: workforce investment justification. Planning decisions: which unique work exceeds sourced productive capacity, which proposed positions or alternatives address that deficit, and which independent continuity/expertise constraints remain unmet.

- Count unique `WorkActivity.workKey` records once. Each has one primary responsibility and optional capability/requirement/NICE many-to-many drivers; mappings do not create hours or positions. Require source/effective date/provisional flag and explicit annual input units. Workload is `(volume × frequency × hours/unit + fixed + project) × growth − distinct outsourced − distinct automated hours`. Reductions cannot exceed gross demand. Divide by a sourced productive-hours-per-FTE input for workload FTE. No organization input receives a fabricated numeric default, including growth or zero reductions. Unknowns remain incomplete.
- Dated assignments link explicit position, incumbent and responsibility. Actual annual capacity is paid/productive full-time hours × FTE allocation × inclusive calendar-day fraction, including leap years. Vacancies/proposals/retired positions and missing/inactive/mismatched incumbents provide no actual capacity. Enter run/change share separately. Serialize assignment/roster edits under the same lock, reject overlapping allocations exceeding position/person limits, and reject position FTE reductions below assignments. Role/task mappings do not imply one job per role.
- Minimum coverage, continuity, expertise and separation-of-duties constraints are evaluated independently of workload. Count unique qualified active assignees and current observed skills; never add coverage headcounts to workload FTE. Skills observations are organization assessments of versioned NICE semantics, not NICE staffing benchmarks. Alternatives/consequences are sourced planning explanations, not formal exception/risk-acceptance workflows.
- Proposed position justifications union linked service responsibilities and unique activities, report demand, available capacity, shortfall, cost/year, business capability/requirement drivers and alternatives/deferral consequences. Shared services across proposals are explicitly not additive. Missing business justification/cost/alternatives remain visible.
- `capacity.ts` owns pure validated methods; server routes own revision409/FK/transaction/history behavior. Migration 007 is additive. Cycle dataset version2 adds an optional captured capacity module and ChangeEvents; older captures never read current workforce inputs into history.
