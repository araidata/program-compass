# Program Compass scope

Program Compass supports cybersecurity strategic planning, capability assessment, and resource planning. Tagline: Cybersecurity strategy and resource planning.

## Phase 0

Establish a deployable application foundation: responsive navigation, header, initialization dashboard, module placeholders, tooling, Prisma configuration, and deployment documentation. No business workflows, authentication implementation, persistence, or organizational metrics are included.

Future modules: Business Context, Current State, Compliance, People & Resources, Technology Portfolio, Strategic Planning, Risks & Findings, Reporting, and Settings.

The organizational model has three pillars: Security Operations; Security Architecture & Engineering; Governance, Risk & Compliance. Future capability, staffing, technology, and planning work must retain this model.

Proposed changes to information architecture, navigation, module naming, and visual direction are under review in [UX_REVIEW.md](UX_REVIEW.md) and tracked in [DECISION_LOG.md](DECISION_LOG.md). They do not change this scope until approved. Current state is in [PROJECT_STATUS.md](PROJECT_STATUS.md).

Architectural requirements are maintained only in [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md).
